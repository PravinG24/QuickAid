How QuickAid works

A streamlined process from request to resolution

1

Submit

Students and staff submit requests through an intuitive form. Include attachments, category, and priority.

2

Triage

Support teams review incoming tickets, assign ownership, and set SLAs based on priority and impact.

3

Update

Teams collaborate and update requesters on progress. Add status changes and resolution notes.

4

Close

Mark tickets as resolved. Requesters confirm satisfaction. Closed tickets remain searchable and auditable.
